## 🧠 How I Approach Problems

<!--
THIS IS ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT SECTIONS.

-->

My general problem-solving process:

1. Understand the problem and constraints.
2. Try to think of a simple/brute-force solution first.
3. Identify where the brute-force approach becomes inefficient.
4. Look for a known pattern or data structure.
5. Develop the optimized approach.
6. Analyze time and space complexity.
7. Implement the solution.
8. Test edge cases and common failure cases.
9. After solving, note down what I learned.
